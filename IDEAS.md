# Theme Ideas for Remaining Placeholder Weeks

Brainstorm document for weeks **25–34** and **36–52** (26 weeks). Weeks **35** (*Preparation for Trumpets*) and **39** (*Trumpets and the Fall Feasts*) are already finalized.

Plan year starts **2026-01-04**. Anchored feast weeks are fixed in `config.js`; everything below assumes the current compile order.

---

## Design Principles (from the plan rules)

- **Canonical arc:** Torah → Prophets → Writings → Gospels → Apostolic writings within each week
- **Messianic continuity:** Yeshua should emerge from the text, not feel bolted on
- **Liturgical fit:** Honor feast seasons where the calendar places them
- **Balanced diet:** Mix narrative, law, wisdom, prophecy, gospel, and epistle
- **No repeats:** Avoid themes already covered in weeks 1–24 (Mark of God pillars, fruit of the Spirit, prayer, fasting, prophecy, dreams/visions, generosity, Shema)

| 20–24 | Preparation for Pentecost, Prophecy, Dreams & Visions, Treasure & Generosity, Shema |
| 35, 39 | Preparation for Trumpets, Trumpets & Fall Feasts |

### Week 48 — Dec 6, 2026

**Selected: Shine Where You Are** *(fruit)* ⭐ *liturgical — Hanukkah*

*You are not called to curse the darkness alone—you are called to carry light into it. Joseph, Esther, and Shadrach show what that looks like in exile, in the palace, and under pressure to bow.*

| Day | Sample chapters | Focus | Application prompt |
|-----|-----------------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | Genesis 41 | Joseph shines before Pharaoh—"God will give the answer" | In your workplace or home, credit God openly for one gift or insight today |
| 2 | Esther 4 | Esther—"for such a time as this" | Name one sphere where only you can act; take one courageous step there |
| 3 | Daniel 3 | Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego refuse the image | Identify one pressure to conform; choose faithfulness over convenience |
| 4 | Matthew 5 | Let your light shine before men | Do one good work visibly—not for applause, but so God gets glory |
| 5 | John 10 | Feast of Dedication—Yeshua, the light no darkness overcomes | When tempted to hide your faith, speak one honest sentence about Him |
| 6 | Ephesians 5 | Walk as children of light; expose darkness | Renounce one secret habit; tell one trusted believer if you need accountability |
| 7 | Philippians 2 | Shine as lights in a crooked generation | Encourage one weary believer; be the lamp they need this week |

**How the stories apply**

| Story | Where they were | How they shined |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Joseph** | Foreign prison → Pharaoh's court | Integrity and excellence; pointed to God instead of self |
| **Esther** | Persian palace, hidden identity | Risked her life when silence would have been safer |
| **Shadrach** | Babylon, imperial pressure | Refused idolatry; trusted God in the fire |

**Alternatives**
- *Hanukkah — Dedication and Light in Darkness* — Haggai 2 → Revelation 1
- *Consecration: Rededicating What Was Defiled* — Daniel 1 → 2 Corinthians 7
- *Gratitude and Joy* — Psalm 100 → Revelation 19

> **Calendar:** Hanukkah 2026 runs **Dec 4–12**. Consider anchoring in `config.js` as `48-shine-where-you-are`.
